# Asset Map

## Wordmarks

Ideal usage: navbar brand.

- `wm.png/svg`: Wordmark that consists of full company and product name in brand colours.
- `wm-b.png/svg`: Wordmark that consists of full company and product name in black.
- `wm-w.png/svg`: Wordmark that consists of full company and product name in white.

## Logo

Ideal usage: favicon, app icon.

- `logo.png/svg`: Logo that consists of the initials of the product name in brand colours.
- `logo-b.png/svg`: Logo that consists of the initials of the product name in black.
- `logo-w.png/svg`: Logo that consists of the initials of the product name in white.
- `logo-square.png/svg`: Logo that consists of the initials of the product name in brand colours in a 1:1 square canvas.
