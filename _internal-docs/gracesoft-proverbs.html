Built to be left alone

Everything it needs, nothing it doesn't

No servers to go down, no accounts to recover, no sync to fail. The app carries everything it needs from the moment it's installed.

Offline, always

Every chapter, in all five translations, ships inside the app. Reading works with no signal and no loading spinner.

No account, ever

Nothing to sign up for and nothing to log into. Your settings live on your device, and only there.

A gentle reminder

Turn on a daily notification and pick the time. It only asks for permission once you switch it on.

Size and shape, your call

A font-size slider and a serif/sans toggle shape the reading screen around your eyes, not a default.

Verse numbers, optional

Read with the numbers showing or switch them off, whichever feels less like a reference book.

Truly free

No ads, no subscription, no in-app purchase. Every translation and every theme is included from install.
